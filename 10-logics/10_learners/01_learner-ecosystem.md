# Learner & Context Research

## 1 Learner Ecosystem

### 1.1 Primary users

The learners who actively complete courses on the platform.

- **Emerging Educators** – Local educators who regularly teach a class or group of learners but have limited or no formal pedagogical training. This includes contract teachers, para-teachers, and community teachers.

### 1.2 Secondary users

People who support, influence, or enable the learning experience rather than completing courses themselves. Their engagement is essential for adoption, implementation, and long-term sustainability.

- **NGO coordinators** – Recruit, onboard, and support Emerging Educators.
- **School leaders and mentors** – Principals, mentor teachers, and other school-based staff who facilitate or influence the implementation of new teaching practices.
- **NGO facilitators/trainers** (if applicable) – Deliver training, coach educators, and may use UnitEd resources in workshops or onboarding.

### 1.3 Future learners (beyond the MVP)

Additional audiences who could benefit from the platform as UnitEd expands.

*Examples: Learning Supporters (community volunteers), Experienced Educators, Parents and caregivers, Youth workers, Community development workers, Early childhood facilitators, International volunteers, Adult Learning Facilities & providers.*
