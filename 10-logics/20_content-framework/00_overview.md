# Content Framework Overview

# Training Topic Research_V2

### **1 Development of the Content Framework**

Seven content categories were developed through a learner-centred design process. Rather than starting from existing teacher training curricula, the framework was derived from the identified learner archetype (“emerging educator”), their Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD), pain points, and the teaching contexts in which they operate ***(see “Learner & Context Research”).***

***Of Note: The content categories currently represent assumptions about learner needs that need to be verified through interviews.***

### **2 Final Content Categories**

**1. Foundation:** 1.1 Teaching & Learning Fundamentals, 1.2 Teacher Presence & Communication, 1.3 Classroom Management  **2 Adaptation:** 2.1 Inclusive Teaching,  2.2 Context-Responsive Teaching  **3. Responsibility & Growth:** 3.1 Ethics & Safeguarding and 3.2 Teacher Growth & Wellbeing
