# Topic Relevance Matrix

#### **Topic Relevance Matrix:**

| Category | MVP Priority | Relevance for Emerging Educators | Expercted Variance within the Teacher Group | Consequence for Content Design |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Foundation** | Very high | High / universal | Low–Medium | Shared foundation for everyone; different entry depths possible |
| 1.1 Teaching & Learning Fundamentals | Very high | Very high | **High** | Common core content, but must accommodate substantially different starting conceptual understanding |
| 1.2 Teacher Presence & Communication | High | High | Medium | Need is universal; starting level varies — one shared entry-level module, with practice tasks that scale in difficulty rather than branching pathways |
| 1.3 Classroom Management | Very high | Very high | Medium | Practice-oriented; account for different class sizes, age groups, and prior exposure |
| **2. Adaptation** | High | High | High | Modularize further; enable context-/learner-specific application |
| 2.1 Inclusive Teaching | High | High | High | Not everything for everyone; different learner needs and contexts as entry points |
| 2.2 Context-Responsive Teaching | High / strategic | High | Very high | Strongly contextualize; examples and strategies must be adaptable to local realities |
| **3. Responsibility & Growth** | Medium–High | High | Medium–High | Core basics for everyone; deeper content later |
| 3.1 Ethics & Safeguarding | **Mandatory** | Very high | Low–Medium | Shared minimum competence; not optional, regardless of variance |
| 3.2 Teacher Growth & Wellbeing | Medium | Medium–High | High | Supportive content; differentiate more strongly by situation and need |
