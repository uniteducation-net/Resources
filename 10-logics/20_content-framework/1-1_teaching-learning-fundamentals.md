# 1.1 Teaching & Learning Fundamentals

#### 1.1 Teaching & Learning Fundamentals

Understanding how learners learn and using evidence-informed teaching practices to plan, deliver, and assess learning. This category provides Emerging Educators with the foundational pedagogical knowledge they need to turn their existing teaching experience into more effective classroom practice.

| Sub-topic | Keep / change | Rationale | Research/search keywords |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **How learning works** | **Merge learning science + memory** | More useful as a practical foundation than teaching separate theoretical concepts | learning science, science of learning, cognitive science, educational psychology, learning processes |
| **Child & adolescent development** | **Keep, but contextualize** | Useful for understanding learners' developmental needs; should remain practical rather than becoming developmental psychology | child development education, adolescent development education, developmental psychology learning |
| **Motivation** | **Keep** | Directly relevant to engagement and persistence; important for classroom practice | student motivation, learner motivation, motivation to learn, self-determination theory education |
| **Active learning** | **Keep / strengthen** | Particularly important for moving Emerging Educators beyond teacher-led instruction | active learning, active learning strategies, experiential learning, inquiry-based learning, student-centred learning |
| **Lesson planning** | **Keep / strengthen** | One of the clearest practical needs identified in your learner research | lesson planning, instructional planning, lesson design, backward design, learning objectives |
| **Assessment & feedback** | **Keep / strengthen** | Essential for knowing whether learners are actually learning | formative assessment, assessment for learning, classroom assessment, feedback, learning assessment |
| **Memory / retrieval** | **Don't make a separate subtopic** | Important, but better integrated into "How learning works" and practical teaching strategies | retrieval practice, spaced learning, spacing effect, memory retention, cognitive load |
