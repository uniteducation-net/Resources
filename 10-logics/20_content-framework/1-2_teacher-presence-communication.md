# 1.2 Teacher Presence & Communication

#### 1.2 Teacher Presence & Communication

Developing the communication and interpersonal skills needed to lead learning effectively. Emerging Educators learn to communicate clearly, give effective instructions and explanations, ask purposeful questions, facilitate participation, and build positive relationships with learners.

| Sub-topic | Keep / change | Rationale | Research/search keywords |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Teacher confidence & self-efficacy** | **Move to Teacher Growth & Wellbeing** | Important, but better understood as a professional development factor than a communication skill | teacher self-efficacy, teacher confidence, teacher self-efficacy beliefs |
| **Teacher presence & non-verbal communication** | **Keep, but narrow** | Useful for educators who may have little formal training, but shouldn't become generic presentation training | teacher presence, teacher nonverbal communication, teacher body language, teacher voice |
| **Giving clear instructions** | **Keep** | Highly practical and directly affects whether learners can participate successfully | instructional clarity, giving instructions, teacher instructions, classroom directions |
| **Explaining & modelling** | **Keep / strengthen** | Core teaching behaviour; connects strongly to effective instruction | teacher explanation, instructional explanation, modelling in teaching, explicit instruction, worked examples |
| **Questioning** | **Keep** | Important for checking understanding, eliciting thinking and moving beyond teacher monologue | teacher questioning, questioning techniques, classroom questioning, higher-order questioning |
| **Facilitating discussions** | **Keep** | Particularly relevant for active and participatory learning | classroom discussion, discussion-based teaching, dialogic teaching, discussion facilitation |
| **Storytelling** | **Move / optional** | Valuable in some contexts, but not sufficiently universal to justify a core Emerging Educator topic | storytelling in education, narrative pedagogy |
| **Teacher–learner relationships** | **Keep** | Important foundation for trust, participation and classroom climate | teacher-student relationships, teacher learner relationship, teacher-student rapport, positive teacher-student interaction |
