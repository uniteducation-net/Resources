# 1.3 Classroom Management

#### 1.3 Classroom Management

Creating a safe, structured and engaging learning environment where learners know what is expected and can participate successfully. Emerging Educators learn proactive strategies for establishing routines, managing behaviour and transitions, organising groups, and maintaining a positive classroom climate.

| Sub-topic | Keep / change | Rationale | Research/search keywords |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Routines & procedures** | **Keep / strengthen** | Foundational for educators with limited formal training; reduces the need to manage every situation reactively | classroom routines, classroom procedures, classroom management routines, instructional routines |
| **Expectations & classroom norms** | **Keep** | Helps Emerging Educators establish predictable and positive learning environments | classroom expectations, classroom norms, behaviour expectations, classroom rules |
| **Positive behaviour management** | **Keep / strengthen** | Core competency; should emphasise proactive and positive approaches rather than punishment | classroom management, positive behaviour support, positive discipline, behaviour management |
| **Transitions** | **Keep** | Particularly important when teaching groups with limited time/resources or large classes | classroom transitions, transition routines, managing transitions |
| **Group work & collaborative learning** | **Keep** | Relevant to organising learners effectively, but overlaps somewhat with Teaching & Learning Fundamentals | cooperative learning, collaborative learning, group work management, managing group work |
| **Learner engagement & participation** | **Keep, but clarify overlap** | Important, but distinguish *creating engagement* from the broader pedagogical concept of active learning | student engagement, learner engagement, student participation, classroom participation |
| **Conflict & de-escalation** | **Keep, but secondary** | Useful practical skill, especially in challenging environments, but not necessarily a foundation for every learner | classroom conflict, conflict resolution education, de-escalation in schools, restorative practices |
| **Classroom climate** | **Keep, but integrate** | Important outcome of routines, relationships and behaviour strategies; doesn't necessarily need to be a standalone module | classroom climate, classroom environment, positive classroom climate |
