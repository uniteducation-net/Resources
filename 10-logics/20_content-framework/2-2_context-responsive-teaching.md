# 2.2 Context-Responsive Teaching

#### 2.2 Context-Responsive Teaching

Adapting teaching practices to the realities in which Emerging Educators work. This includes making effective use of limited resources, responding to local languages and cultures, working with families and communities, and adapting teaching to challenging or under-resourced environments.

| **Sub-topic** | **Keep / change** | **Rationale** | **Research/search keywords** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Low-resource & low-bandwidth teaching** | **Keep / strengthen** | Highly relevant to Emerging Educators working with limited materials, connectivity, or infrastructure. | low-resource teaching, resource-constrained classrooms, low-bandwidth teaching, teaching with limited resources, teaching without materials |
| **Large & under-resourced classes** | **Keep** | Directly addresses classroom realities identified for the target group, particularly large or overcrowded classes. | large class teaching, overcrowded classrooms, teaching large classes, under-resourced schools |
| **Culturally responsive teaching** | **Keep** | Helps educators connect teaching to learners' cultural backgrounds and local realities. | culturally responsive teaching, culturally sustaining pedagogy, culturally relevant pedagogy, cultural responsiveness in education |
| **Community-based education** | **Keep** | Relevant where education is closely connected to communities and formal school structures may be limited. | community-based education, community education, non-formal education, community learning |
| **Family & community engagement** | **Keep** | Supports educators in working with the families and communities that shape learners' educational experiences. | family engagement, parent engagement, community engagement, school-community partnerships |
| **Contextualising learning materials** | **Keep / strengthen** | Particularly relevant for adapting materials and curriculum to local languages, cultures, resources, and learner realities. | contextualised teaching, contextualising learning materials, curriculum adaptation, locally relevant education, adapting teaching to local contexts |
| **Teaching in challenging contexts** | **Keep, but secondary** | Important for some Emerging Educators, particularly those working in humanitarian, crisis-affected, or fragile settings, but not universal across the group. | education in emergencies, teaching in humanitarian settings, crisis-affected education, teaching in fragile contexts |
