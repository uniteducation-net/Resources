# 3.1 Ethics & Safeguarding

#### 3.1 Ethics & Safeguarding

Understanding the responsibilities of educators to protect learners’ safety, dignity, rights, and wellbeing. Emerging Educators develop the knowledge and practical skills to maintain appropriate professional boundaries, recognise safeguarding concerns, and respond appropriately according to relevant procedures.

| **Sub-topic** | **Keep / change** | **Rationale** | **Research/search keywords** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Child Protection & Safeguarding** | **Keep / core** | Fundamental competency for anyone with regular responsibility for children and young people. | child safeguarding, child protection, safeguarding in education, child protection education |
| **Professional Boundaries** | **Keep / strengthen** | Emerging Educators may have limited formal preparation for navigating power relationships and appropriate adult–learner interactions. | professional boundaries in education, teacher professional boundaries, educator conduct, teacher-student boundaries |
| **Power & Responsibility** | **Keep, but integrate** | Helps educators understand the power imbalance inherent in their role and its ethical implications. | power dynamics in education, teacher power, educator ethics, teacher-student power relationships |
