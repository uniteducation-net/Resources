# 3.2 Teacher Growth & Wellbeing

#### 3.2 Teacher Growth & Wellbeing

Supporting Emerging Educators in sustaining their motivation, wellbeing, and professional development while working in often under-supported teaching environments. This includes reflecting on practice, recognising areas for improvement, building confidence and resilience, and developing supportive peer networks.

| **Sub-topic** | **Keep / change** | **Rationale** | **Research/search keywords** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Reflective Practice** | **Keep / strengthen** | A core mechanism for turning teaching experience into professional learning, particularly where formal mentoring is limited. | reflective practice teachers, reflective teaching, teacher reflection, reflective practitioner education |
| **Self-Evaluation & Professional Growth** | **Keep** | Helps Emerging Educators identify strengths, gaps, and areas for continued development without relying entirely on formal training. | teacher self-evaluation, teacher self-assessment, teacher professional development, teacher professional learning |
| **Peer Learning & Support** | **Keep / strengthen** | Particularly relevant for educators who may have limited access to formal mentors or professional learning communities. | teacher peer learning, peer coaching teachers, teacher peer support, professional learning communities, teacher collaboration |
| **Confidence & Self-Efficacy** | **Move here from Teacher Presence & Communication** | Confidence is an important developmental outcome, but is better addressed as professional self-efficacy than as a communication skill. | teacher self-efficacy, teacher confidence, teacher self-belief, educator self-efficacy |
| **Resilience & Role-Related Stress** | **Keep, contextualise** | Relevant to educators working with limited support and resources; avoid framing wellbeing primarily around workload or burnout. | teacher resilience, educator resilience, teacher stress, role-related stress teachers, teacher wellbeing |
| **Isolation & Support Networks** | **Keep** | Particularly relevant where Emerging Educators work in remote, community-based, or under-supported settings. | teacher isolation, rural teacher isolation, teacher support networks, peer support educators, teacher social support |
| **Burnout** | **Keep, but secondary** | Relevant but should not dominate the category; burnout is not necessarily the defining wellbeing challenge for this heterogeneous group. | teacher burnout, educator burnout, teacher wellbeing, occupational wellbeing teachers |
