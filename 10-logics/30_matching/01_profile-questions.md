# Profile Questions

Asked in the app's start chat, one at a time. The answers become the
teacher's profile and the state Jev evaluates.

## The five questions

1. What age group will you be teaching?
2. Have you worked with children before?
3. Describe your work or academic background in short.
4. What will you be teaching and where?
5. What is your target schedule and time commitment for studying with UnitEd?

## What each answer informs

| Answer | Informs |
|---|---|
| Age group | which entry depth and examples fit — see the variance columns of [[Topic Relevance Matrix]] |
| Worked with children | entry depth for the Foundation categories |
| Background | starting conceptual understanding — the high-variance axis of [[1.1 Teaching & Learning Fundamentals]] |
| What and where | contextualisation needs — [[2.2 Context-Responsive Teaching]] |
| Schedule and commitment | pacing — how much one step may ask at once |
