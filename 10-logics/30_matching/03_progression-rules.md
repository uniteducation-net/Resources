# Progression Rules

How a teacher moves through steps. One step at a time — never a syllabus
dump.

## The workspace

Every teacher's workspace holds only:

1. **Profile** — who they are, growing with every answered question.
2. **Start here** — how the system works, on one page, simple enough that a
   ten-year-old understands it.
3. **Step folders** — numbered, one per step, each holding exactly one
   suggested resource.

## The next-step protocol

Triggered by the "open my next step" button on a Step file:

1. Save the current chat.
2. Ask the minimal standard questions — one at a time, super short — about
   how the last step went and what they want next. Save the answers into the
   Profile.
3. Re-query this repo: Jev re-evaluates with the updated profile as the
   state (see [[Jev Evaluation]]).
4. Suggest exactly one resource. NEVER suggest a step the teacher has
   already done — the done list is the Step folders that already exist.
5. Write the next numbered Step folder with that one resource and the
   next-step button.

## Editing

The app may update the workspace, and the teacher may update it too — the
workspace is theirs. Files are markdown; nothing is locked.
