# 30_matching — how a profile picks the next step

One job: the operating rules that turn a teacher's answers into the next
single step.

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `01_profile-questions.md` | the five onboarding questions and what each one informs |
| `02_jev-evaluation.md` | the typed questions Jev evaluates against those answers |
| `03_progression-rules.md` | one step at a time, never repeat, how the next step is chosen |

These are authored operating rules — the only part of `10-logics/` that is
not verbatim research. They implement the research, they do not replace it:
where they disagree with `../10_learners/` or `../20_content-framework/`,
the research wins and these files change.
