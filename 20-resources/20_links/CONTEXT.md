# 20_links — third-party resources

One job: hold pointers to single external resources worth a step — a page, a
PDF, a tool — with enough context to decide fit without opening them.

## Rules

- Stamp from `../../_templates/external-link.md`.
- One link per file. The summary says what the teacher gets and why it is
  worth their time.
- Verify the link loads before marking `status: approved`.
- Sources with many useful parts are not links — they are
  `../30_providers/`.
