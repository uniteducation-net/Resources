# Classroom Management Idea Book

A free, practical handbook written for new volunteer teachers working in
under-resourced schools around the world, with contributions from
classrooms in Malawi, Nepal, Mozambique, Jordan and elsewhere. In plain
language it covers how to set up classroom routines and expectations, keep
every learner engaged, make full use of lesson time, and handle disruptive
behaviour positively — including how to build order in schools where
corporal punishment is the norm. Because it assumes no prior teacher
training, every strategy is concrete, step-by-step and immediately usable.

## What the teacher gets

- Step-by-step guidance for establishing classroom routines, procedures and behaviour expectations
- Concrete strategies to keep all learners engaged and make full use of limited lesson time
- Positive, non-violent approaches to managing disruptive behaviour and giving discipline
- Cross-cultural advice for adapting classroom management to local school norms and gaining colleagues' support

*Access: free direct PDF download (2.5 MB, 136 pages) from peacecorps.gov; no login. English. One download, then fully readable offline on a phone.*
