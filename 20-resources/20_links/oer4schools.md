# OER4Schools Professional Learning Resource

A complete, free professional learning programme built for teachers in
low-resource African primary schools, co-authored by Cambridge researchers
and practising Zambian teachers. Through 28 practical two-hour sessions it
moves teachers from chalk-and-talk to interactive teaching: effective
questioning, whole-class dialogue, group work, assessment for learning,
lesson pacing, and enquiry-based learning. Every session gives concrete
classroom activities and case examples from real Zambian classrooms, so a
teacher with no formal training can try one idea at a time in their own
class. It can be worked through alone, with a colleague, or as a school
group — no trainer required.

## What the teacher gets

- Step-by-step session plans for interactive, student-centred teaching that work with or without technology
- Practical techniques for questioning, whole-class dialogue, and managing group work in large classes
- Simple assessment-for-learning methods to check understanding and pace lessons
- Real classroom examples and videos from Zambian and South African schools, plus facilitator notes for peer study

*Access: free, no login. Lightweight HTML pages; optional PDFs and offline ZIM/Kiwix downloads for intermittent connectivity. Videos are optional extras. English. CC BY-SA / CC BY-NC.*
