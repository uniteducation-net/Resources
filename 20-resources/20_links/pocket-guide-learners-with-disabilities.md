# Pocket Guide to Supporting Learners with Disabilities

A short, illustrated pocket guide from INEE that helps teachers make their
classroom welcoming to children with disabilities and other additional
learning needs, even with almost no materials or training. It was written
for teachers in low-resource and crisis-affected settings, so every
suggestion assumes large classes, few supplies, and no specialist support.
Teachers get simple explanations of why children may be left out, then
step-by-step ideas for adapting lessons, seating, and activities so every
learner can join in. It works well for self-study: a teacher can read a
few pages at a time and try one idea the next day.

## What the teacher gets

- Practical checklists of low- and no-cost ways to make lessons and the classroom more inclusive
- Guidance on spotting barriers to learning and noticing which children need extra support
- Step-by-step ideas for adapting activities, instructions, and learning spaces for different needs
- Self-study format with illustrations, usable without a trainer or internet connection

*Access: free PDF (1.5 MB, 82 pages), no login. English. Downloads once, readable offline.*
