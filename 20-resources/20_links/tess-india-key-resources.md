# TESS-India Key Resources for Teachers

A free, open-licensed toolkit of ten short "key resources" on core
classroom practice from The Open University, written with Indian educators
for teachers working in large, under-resourced classrooms with little
formal training. It includes dedicated units on using questioning to
promote thinking, talk for learning (classroom discussion), pair and group
work, and involving all learners — with concrete routines, example
questions and worked examples rather than theory. Used by over a million
teachers, it gives an emerging educator immediately usable techniques for
how they speak, question and respond to learners.

## What the teacher gets

- Ready-to-use questioning techniques: wait time, prompting, probing, and sequencing questions from recall to reasoning
- Routines to get every learner talking: hot-seating, question walls, and planning purposeful talk in any subject
- Ways to respond positively to wrong or partial answers so learners keep trying
- Practical structures for pair and group work and for noticing and including quiet or marginalised learners

*Access: free, no login. Lightweight HTML pages; optional PDF/EPUB/Word downloads for offline use. English (other-language versions exist). CC BY-NC-SA.*
