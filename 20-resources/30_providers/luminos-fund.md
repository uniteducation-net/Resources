# Luminos Fund Community Teachers

The Luminos Fund — an education NGO whose programmes train local young
people as community teachers to run catch-up learning classes for
out-of-school children.

## What we take

- *Community Teachers* (page in the Luminos Method): how community teachers
  are recruited, trained, and supported:
  https://luminosfund.org/luminos-method/community-teachers/

## When we suggest it

- When a teacher's context is community-based education with little formal
  structure — a real-world picture of the [[The Emerging Educator]] reality.
