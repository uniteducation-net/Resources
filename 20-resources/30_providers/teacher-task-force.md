# Teacher Task Force

The International Task Force on Teachers for Education 2030 — a global
alliance hosted by UNESCO that publishes research and policy guidance on the
teacher workforce, including contract, community, and para-teachers.

## What we take

- *Contract teachers in sub-Saharan Africa* (report PDF): the definitions
  and typology sections describing who contract teachers are and how they
  are employed:
  https://teachertaskforce.org/sites/default/files/2020-07/Contract%20teachers%20in%20sub-Saharan%20Africa_EN.pdf

## When we suggest it

- Grounding on teacher types when a profile or partner question touches
  employment status, training, or teacher shortages — see
  [[Types of Teachers]].
