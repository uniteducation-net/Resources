# Teacher Wellbeing Resource Collection

A curated library of free, practical tools on teacher wellbeing from INEE,
the global network for education in emergencies, built specifically for
teachers working in crisis-affected and under-resourced settings. The
collection gathers ready-to-use items in one place: guidance notes,
workshop packs, self-checks, and training packs — several adapted to
contexts like Kakuma refugee camp, Colombia, Myanmar, and Palestine.

## What we take

- One small tool at a time, named per step — for example the plain-language
  guidance note on resilience and role-related stress, the WHO-5
  five-question wellbeing self-check, or the peer workshop pack
  (facilitation guide, slides, workbooks).
- The app fetches the collection page live and serves only the named tool.
  The whole library is never mirrored.

## When we suggest it

- Steps in [[3.2 Teacher Growth & Wellbeing]]: resilience and role-related
  stress, isolation and support networks, peer learning and support,
  reflective self-checks.

*Access: free, no login. Lightweight HTML index linking to small PDF
downloads. English; flagship items in 6+ additional languages.*
