# UK Government Contract Teachers Research

Research published via the UK government's publishing service on contract
teachers in developing countries (Kingdon et al., 2012 protocol) — who they
are, how they are hired, and what the evidence says.

## What we take

- *Contract Teachers* protocol (PDF): the background and definitions
  sections on contract teacher employment:
  https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/57a08a9940f0b652dd0007e6/Contract_Teachers2012Kingdon_Protocol.pdf

## When we suggest it

- Deeper evidence context behind teacher hiring patterns — supports
  [[Types of Teachers]].
