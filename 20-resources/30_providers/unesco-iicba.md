# UNESCO IICBA

The UNESCO International Institute for Capacity Building in Africa (IICBA) —
works on teacher education and development across Africa and publishes
explainers on the different kinds of teachers in the workforce.

## What we take

- *A Different Kind of Teacher* (web page): the descriptions of teacher
  categories — contract, community, and para-teachers — and why they exist:
  https://www.iicba.unesco.org/en/different-kind-teacher

## When we suggest it

- When a step calls for a short, readable orientation on teacher categories
  in African education systems — see [[Types of Teachers]].
