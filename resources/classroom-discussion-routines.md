# Classroom Discussion Routines

Four named routines that get every student talking — not just the confident
few. Each fits neatly into the guided-practice part of a
[[Lesson Plan Template]].

## Think–Pair–Share

Pose a question. Students think silently (1 min), discuss with a partner
(2 min), then share with the class. **Use when:** you want 100%
participation on a question worth discussing.

## Four Corners

Label the room's corners with four positions (e.g. strongly agree →
strongly disagree). Read a statement; students move to a corner and defend
it to their group. **Use when:** the topic has real disagreement and you
want movement plus argument.

## Numbered Heads Together

Groups of four, numbered 1–4. Groups discuss a question until everyone can
answer it — then you call a number, and that student answers for the group.
**Use when:** you need accountability; nobody can hide.

## Whip Around

A fast pass around the room: each student says one word or one sentence in
response to a prompt. Passing is allowed. **Use when:** closing a lesson or
warming up — two minutes, everyone heard.

## Tips

- Name the routine before you start it. Students learn the moves once and
  you save instructions all year.
- Cold-call gently after pair time, not before — the pair step is what
  makes cold-calling safe.
