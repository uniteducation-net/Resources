# Lesson Plan Template

A reusable five-part lesson structure that works for any subject and grade
level. Fill each part in your own words — a rough plan you trust beats a
perfect plan you don't.

## The five parts

### 1. Hook (5 min)

Open with something that makes the topic matter *today*: a question, a
short story, a surprising fact, a quick demonstration. One goal — curiosity.

### 2. Direct instruction (10–15 min)

Teach the new idea in one clear pass. One idea per lesson. Show a worked
example while thinking out loud.

### 3. Guided practice (10–15 min)

Students try the skill with support — in pairs, as a class, or with you
circulating. This is the moment for talk: see
[[Classroom Discussion Routines]] for structures that get everyone
participating, not just the usual three hands.

### 4. Independent work (10–15 min)

Students apply the skill alone. Keep the task small enough to finish and
specific enough to check.

### 5. Closure (5 min)

Land the plane: one sentence summaries, an exit ticket question, or a
preview of what comes next. End on the idea, not the bell.

## Notes

- Timings are guides, not rules. Double-block? Run the cycle twice.
- If a part runs long, cut from direct instruction first — practice is
  where learning happens.
